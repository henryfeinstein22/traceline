What Traceline does

Seven things, one product

🎓

Built-in AI literacy

Every few messages, a short age-appropriate tip teaches how AI actually works — not a supervision feature, the actual point.

🧒

Scales with age

Elementary kids get simple, friendly guidance. Middle and high schoolers get real AI-literacy concepts. Same product, different depth.

🧭

Decisions & routines

A mode for everyday choices and planning — the AI asks questions and lays out tradeoffs, but never decides for the kid.

🛡️

Parent visibility

Every message your kid sends and every AI reply is logged and safety-checked in real time. No black box.

📝

Homework provenance

Turn on homework mode and the conversation becomes a shareable draft trail — proof of process, not just a final essay.

🚩

Real-time flags

Self-harm language, personal info sharing, or dangerous requests are flagged the moment they happen — and a parent alert fires immediately.

🔗

Shareable with teachers

A read-only provenance link for any homework conversation — the same evidence academic-integrity reviews already ask for.
